## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-30

### Added

- A terminal Rubik's Cube timer with WCA random-state 3x3 scrambles.
- Hold-to-start timing with a tap-to-start fallback, optional WCA inspection,
  penalties, solve details, sessions, themes, and persistent SQLite history.
- WCA-style statistics including single, mean, mo3, ao5, ao12, ao100, personal
  bests, progress chart, cube net, scramble preview, and interactive 3D cube.
- Standard command-line help and version output.
